Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit feedback?

Click the "Citi Snap" button in the bottom right corner, select your role, and click "Give Feedback". Fill out the form and submit.

Can I attach screenshots?

Yes! Click "Capture Screenshot" in the feedback form to automatically capture the current page.

How do I search for previous feedback?

Use the Search shortcut to find feedback by description, role, ticket ID, or date range.

Where is my feedback stored?

All feedback is stored locally in an Excel file (tickets.xlsx) and screenshots are saved in the screenshots folder.

Can I export my feedback data?

Yes! Use the Export shortcut to download your feedback data in CSV or JSON format.
